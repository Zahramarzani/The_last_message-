A Message from Tomorrow

A dead transmission relay begins clicking. Maya’s phone plays the final part of Eli’s message.

ELI: “The relay’s clock was misconfigured. The timestamp is wrong. But the danger is real.”

Eli explains that railway executives suppressed an accident report. He fled after the company threatened Maya and Helen. The message contains an address where he might be found.

Maya compares the archive with the records she collected. Names and payments match: the evidence is strong.

The Truth Has a Price
